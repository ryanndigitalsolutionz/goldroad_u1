# GoldRoad U1 — Privacy Policy

**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GoldRoad U1 collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when users access or use GoldRoad U1 and its related services.

GoldRoad U1 is designed to provide a unified account identity across its platform and related tools.

The information collected is limited to what GoldRoad U1 reasonably requires to provide its services, maintain accounts, process transactions, support users, protect the platform, and operate connected GoldRoad U1 services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

When creating or using a GoldRoad U1 account, GoldRoad U1 may collect:

- email address;
- phone number;
- profile information; and
- the information necessary to create and maintain the user's GoldRoad U1 account.

### 2.2 U1 ID

GoldRoad U1 assigns an individual account a unique **U1 ID**.

The U1 ID is used to identify and synchronize the user's account across applicable GoldRoad U1 services and tools.

Where related GoldRoad U1 tools are connected to the user's account, the U1 ID may be used to associate the user's account and permitted information across those services.

A U1 ID does not by itself disclose the user's identity to other users.

### 2.3 Identity Verification Information

GoldRoad U1 may collect additional identity information where account eligibility, service access, security, or another legitimate platform requirement requires verification.

This may include information from an accepted identity document or other verification information requested by GoldRoad U1.

### 2.4 Transaction Information

When users purchase P-n, subscriptions, advertising services, or other applicable services, GoldRoad U1 may record information concerning the transaction, including:

- transaction identifiers;
- purchased services or bundles;
- amounts;
- currency;
- P-n activity;
- transaction status;
- refund activity; and
- relevant dates and timestamps.

Payment processing may be performed through third-party payment providers.

GoldRoad U1 maintains its own transaction records for transactions conducted through its platform.

### 2.5 Service and Usage Information

GoldRoad U1 may collect information generated through the use of its services, including information concerning:

- services requested or used;
- advertising activity;
- freelancing activity;
- account activity;
- platform interactions;
- support requests; and
- security or enforcement events.

### 2.6 Device and Technical Information

GoldRoad U1 may process technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the platform.

This may include information such as:

- device or browser information;
- IP address;
- network information;
- session information;
- timestamps;
- diagnostic information; and
- security-related information.

The exact information collected depends on the functionality being used.

## 3. How We Use Information

GoldRoad U1 may use personal information to:

- create and maintain user accounts;
- provide GoldRoad U1 services;
- assign and maintain U1 IDs;
- synchronize permitted account information across connected GoldRoad U1 services;
- process purchases and transactions;
- administer P-n and subscriptions;
- provide advertising and freelancing functionality;
- communicate with users;
- respond to support requests;
- verify accounts and identities where required;
- protect the platform and users;
- detect and investigate misuse or suspicious activity;
- maintain transaction and operational records;
- improve the reliability and functionality of GoldRoad U1; and
- comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

## 4. Account Synchronization

GoldRoad U1 may use the U1 ID as a common account identifier across GoldRoad U1 services.

This allows a user to maintain a consistent account identity where multiple GoldRoad U1 tools are connected.

Connected services will receive only the information reasonably necessary for the functionality being provided.

A user's information is not automatically shared with every GoldRoad U1 service merely because both services are owned or operated under GoldRoad U1.

## 5. O.M.L.I and Sage-Guide

O.M.L.I and Sage-Guide may operate as separate GoldRoad U1 tools or applications where made available.

Where a user connects such a service to their GoldRoad U1 account, GoldRoad U1 may use the user's U1 ID and other permitted account information to provide account synchronization and related functionality.

The information shared with a connected service depends on the functionality that service provides.

## 6. P-n and Subscription Information

GoldRoad U1 records information necessary to administer P-n purchases, P-n usage, subscriptions, and related transactions.

P-n transaction information may be associated with the user's U1 ID so that GoldRoad U1 can maintain the user's applicable balance, transaction history, service usage, and refund records.

Specific rules governing P-n purchases and subscriptions are described in the GoldRoad U1 P-n Purchase and Subscription Policy.

## 7. Advertising and Freelancing Information

Information submitted or generated through GoldRoad U1 advertising and freelancing services may be processed to provide those services.

This may include information relating to:

- advertising submissions;
- advertising campaigns;
- freelance jobs;
- proposals;
- accepted transaction terms;
- communications concerning a service; and
- transaction or service status.

Users remain responsible for information and content they submit through the platform.

## 8. Communications

GoldRoad U1 may use the user's email address, phone number, or other permitted communication channels to provide:

- account communications;
- service updates;
- transaction notifications;
- security notifications;
- support responses;
- important changes to GoldRoad U1 services or policies; and
- other communications necessary to operate the platform.

## 9. Information Sharing

GoldRoad U1 may share personal information where reasonably necessary to provide services or operate the platform.

Recipients may include:

- payment providers;
- hosting and infrastructure providers;
- communications providers;
- media or content-processing providers;
- advertising or publishing providers;
- authentication or identity-verification providers;
- security and technical service providers; and
- other service providers acting on behalf of GoldRoad U1.

GoldRoad U1 does not sell a user's personal information merely because the information is processed by a service provider.

## 10. Connected GoldRoad U1 Services

Where a user chooses to use a connected GoldRoad U1 service, information may be shared between applicable GoldRoad U1 systems as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Such sharing remains subject to this Privacy Policy and the applicable service terms.

## 11. Data Retention

GoldRoad U1 may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information and its purpose, including:

- account administration;
- transaction records;
- security records;
- support history;
- contractual relationships; and
- operational or legal requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore be retained for different periods.

## 12. Data Security

GoldRoad U1 uses reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No information system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are also responsible for maintaining reasonable security over their account credentials and devices.

## 13. Local Storage and Offline Functionality

GoldRoad U1 may store selected information locally on a user's device to support functionality such as preferences, previously available content, saved items, drafts, or offline access.

Local storage may use browser or application technologies such as cookies, session storage, IndexedDB, or the Cache API where implemented.

Locally stored information may become outdated.

Server-side GoldRoad U1 records remain authoritative for account information, transactions, P-n, payments, and other information requiring current platform authority.

Additional details concerning cookies, sessions, and local storage are provided in the GoldRoad U1 Cookies, Sessions & Local Storage Policy.

## 14. User Rights and Choices

Users may contact GoldRoad U1 concerning their personal information, account information, or applicable privacy rights.

Depending on the user's circumstances and applicable requirements, GoldRoad U1 may provide mechanisms to:

- access account information;
- correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion where applicable;
- manage account information; or
- raise concerns regarding the processing of personal information.

Some information may need to be retained where GoldRoad U1 has a legitimate operational, security, contractual, or other applicable reason to retain it.

## 15. Account Closure

Where a user closes or terminates a GoldRoad U1 account, GoldRoad U1 may deactivate the account while retaining certain information as reasonably necessary for transaction history, security, dispute resolution, operational continuity, or other applicable purposes.

Account closure does not automatically erase historical transaction records.

## 16. Third-Party Services

GoldRoad U1 may use third-party services to operate portions of the platform.

Third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

GoldRoad U1 may change or replace third-party providers as its services evolve.

## 17. International Processing

GoldRoad U1 may use service providers or infrastructure located in jurisdictions other than the user's location.

Where personal information is processed across jurisdictions, GoldRoad U1 will apply the safeguards and requirements applicable to the relevant processing.

## 18. Children

GoldRoad U1 is intended for users who satisfy the minimum eligibility requirements established in the GoldRoad U1 Terms of Service.

GoldRoad U1 does not intentionally design ordinary account access for individuals who do not meet those requirements.

## 19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

GoldRoad U1 may update this Privacy Policy as its services, technology, and data practices evolve.

Where appropriate, GoldRoad U1 may provide notice of material changes through available platform or communication channels.

The effective date shown at the beginning of this Privacy Policy identifies the current version.

## 20. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns concerning privacy or personal information may be submitted through the designated GoldRoad U1 privacy or support contact channels.

The applicable contact information will be made available through the GoldRoad U1 platform.
