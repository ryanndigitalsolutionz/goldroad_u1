# GoldRoad U1 — Statement of Work

****Document Status:**** Draft  

****Document Type:**** Statement of Work (SOW)  

****Platform:**** GoldRoad U1  

****Governing Agreement:**** GoldRoad U1 Master Services Agreement (MSA)

## 1. Purpose

This Statement of Work ("SOW") defines the operational scope of services provided through GoldRoad U1.

GoldRoad U1 is a remote SaaS commercial platform through which businesses, professionals, sellers, producers, and other participants can use platform services for:

1\. Freelancing;

2\. Principal reselling / Service-Specific Commerce; and

3\. Advertising.

This SOW operates together with the GoldRoad U1 Master Services Agreement ("MSA"). The MSA establishes the overarching contractual, legal, commercial, security, payment, intellectual-property, liability, and other governing terms. This SOW describes how the applicable GoldRoad U1 services are intended to operate.

Where this SOW conflicts with the MSA, the order of precedence established by the MSA applies unless the applicable SOW expressly states otherwise.

## 2. Current Active Scope

For this current SOW, GoldRoad U1's active service scope is limited to:

1. **Freelancing** — facilitating professional skills, services, and production work.
2. **Advertising** — enabling creation, management, placement, distribution, or facilitation of advertising and promotional activity.

Product commerce, specialized infrastructure, operational coordination, specialized services, and related commerce-logistics capabilities are outside the current SOW and are not offered as active services under it. Any future capability will require its own expressly applicable scope and documentation before it is offered.

## 3. Parties and Platform Relationship

GoldRoad U1 functions as the platform and commercial facilitation layer between participants.

### 2.1 Freelancing

****Freelancer → GoldRoad U1 → Client****

The freelancer uses GoldRoad U1 to connect with and serve a client. The freelancer is responsible for delivering the agreed professional service to the client.

### 2.3 Advertising

****Business/Advertiser → GoldRoad U1 → Target Audience****

A business or advertiser uses GoldRoad U1 to create, publish, or distribute advertising through supported GoldRoad U1 advertising capabilities and, where eligible and supported, external advertising channels.

GoldRoad U1 may provide:

Availability may depend on account status, subscription level, geography, regulation, third-party services, technical availability, and current product configuration.

## 5. Freelancing Service

### 4.1 Service Description

GoldRoad U1 provides a marketplace and facilitation environment through which freelancers can connect with clients requiring professional services.

GoldRoad U1 does not, merely by hosting the transaction, become the direct provider of an independently contracted professional service. The freelancer remains responsible for performing and delivering the service agreed with the client.

### 4.2 Freelancing Categories

The freelancing market consists of five primary categories:

1\. Creative & Media Production;

2\. Web Dev & Digital Infrastructure;

3\. Performance Market & Traffic;

4\. Copywriting & Content Strategy; and

5\. Strategic Brokerage & Deal-making.

Specific services within these categories may change as GoldRoad U1 evolves.

### 4.3 Client Job Specification

A client may establish a job/project specification containing:

- Project or service description;

- Specific problem or need;

- Required deliverables;

- Prerequisites;

- Required freelancer level or experience;

- Proposed completion period;

- Proposed price or budget; and

- Other technical or commercial requirements.

The client is responsible for providing sufficiently clear requirements.

### 4.4 Negotiation

A freelancer may negotiate with the client concerning applicable transaction terms, including price, completion period, scope, deliverables, and other mutually negotiable conditions.

The final terms accepted by both parties constitute the operative transaction terms for the engagement. Neither party should rely on material requirements that were not communicated and accepted.

### 4.5 Freelancer Responsibilities

The freelancer is responsible for:

- Understanding accepted requirements;

- Providing the agreed professional service;

- Meeting the accepted delivery period;

- Providing agreed deliverables;

- Completing the agreed hand-over;

- Respecting applicable intellectual-property and confidentiality obligations;

- Maintaining truthful professional information;

- Complying with GoldRoad U1 platform rules; and

- Cooperating with legitimate dispute investigations.

### 4.6 Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for:

- Providing sufficiently clear project requirements;

- Providing prerequisites or information reasonably necessary for completion;

- Respecting the accepted scope and deadline;

- Paying the agreed transaction amount;

- Not deliberately changing material terms after acceptance;

- Not improperly withholding payment;

- Not demanding material additional work outside the accepted scope without agreement;

- Respecting applicable intellectual-property and confidentiality obligations; and

- Cooperating with legitimate dispute investigations.

### 4.7 Delivery and Acceptance

A freelancer performs the engagement according to the accepted transaction terms when the agreed deliverables and requirements have been substantially satisfied and properly handed over.

Where the parties define an objective acceptance process, that process governs. A client may not reject or materially alter an otherwise conforming deliverable solely because of a subjective preference that was not part of the accepted requirements.

### 4.8 Freelancing Complaints

Either party may submit a complaint where the other party is alleged to have materially breached accepted transaction terms or applicable GoldRoad U1 rules.

Client-side violations may include refusal to pay after conforming delivery, material underpayment, deliberate evasion of an accepted payment obligation, improper attempts to accelerate the agreed deadline, deliberate obstruction of completion, or other material transaction abuse.

Freelancer-side violations may include failure to deliver agreed work, material failure to satisfy accepted requirements, unauthorized demands for additional payment, failure to provide agreed deliverables, unauthorized retention of material portions of a deliverable, deliberate sabotage of delivered work, or other material transaction abuse.

### 4.9 Complaint Evidence

A complaint may include screenshots, video recordings, audio recordings, messages, interview or negotiation records, accepted transaction specifications, payment records, delivery records, and other relevant evidence.

GoldRoad U1 may review accepted transaction terms and available evidence before determining whether platform enforcement is appropriate.

### 4.10 Enforcement

Where GoldRoad U1 determines that a material platform or transaction violation occurred, it may apply applicable enforcement measures, including:

- Warnings;

- Financial penalties;

- P-n / Pin-point deductions where applicable;

- Account restrictions;

- Suspension;

- Loss of platform privileges;

- Permanent account termination; or

- Other measures permitted by the MSA and applicable platform rules.

Internal enforcement thresholds, scoring mechanisms, and penalty algorithms may remain undisclosed.

### 4.11 Freelancing Limitations

GoldRoad U1 does not guarantee:

- That a freelancer will receive a job;

- That a client will find a suitable freelancer;

- A freelancer's professional competence;

- A client's subjective satisfaction beyond accepted requirements;

- A particular commercial outcome;

- That independently provided work will be free from defects;

- That a project will produce revenue or other business results; or

- Continuous availability of every freelancer or client.

## 6. Advertising Service

### 6.1 Service Description

GoldRoad U1 provides businesses with a simplified and cost-efficient alternative for promoting businesses, products, and services to audiences across supported markets.

Advertising may be made available through GoldRoad U1's own capabilities and, where eligible and supported, external advertising channels.

### 6.2 Advertising Formats

Subject to current availability, advertising may include:

- Image advertisements;

- Promotional posters;

- Popup advertisements;

- Timed advertisements;

- Video advertisements; and

- External advertising distribution.

Specific formats, pricing, requirements, and availability are governed by applicable advertising documentation and GoldRoad U1 configuration.

### 6.3 Advertising Requirements

Advertisers must provide materials and information satisfying applicable GoldRoad U1 requirements, content rules, technical specifications, payment requirements, P-n requirements, publishing requirements, external advertising-platform requirements, and applicable law.

Video advertising intended for external publication may be subject to additional account, rating, eligibility, payment, or publishing requirements.

### 6.4 Advertising Limitations

GoldRoad U1 facilitates advertising visibility but does not guarantee a particular number of impressions, clicks, leads, sales, conversions, search rankings, audience size, engagement, revenue, or other commercial outcomes unless expressly stated in applicable advertising terms.

## 7. Platform Payments and P-n

### 7.1 Payment Processing

GoldRoad U1 may integrate multiple payment providers and payment methods. Availability may depend on country, currency, customer eligibility, payment provider, regulatory requirements, transaction type, and technical availability.

Payment processing may be performed by third-party payment providers. GoldRoad U1 may maintain its own transaction records independently of provider records.

### 7.2 P-n / Pin-points

GoldRoad U1 may use P-n / Pin-points as a platform utility or virtual currency for eligible platform functions.

****6.5 P-n = USD 1****

P-n may be consumed for eligible platform functions according to current GoldRoad U1 pricing and subscription rules.

P-n must not be traded, exchanged, or converted into unauthorized cash or unauthorized platform benefits.

### 7.3 Subscription and P-n Rules

Subscription plans, P-n allocations, pricing, grace periods, expiration rules, and applicable benefits are governed by current subscription terms and the MSA. This SOW does not override those terms unless expressly stated.

## 8. Third-Party Services

GoldRoad U1 may depend on third-party services for payment processing, cloud storage, media delivery, external advertising, communications, identity/account services, analytics, and other infrastructure.

GoldRoad U1 may change, replace, add, or remove providers where reasonably necessary to operate or improve the platform.

Third-party services may have separate terms, pricing, availability requirements, geographic limitations, and interruptions.

GoldRoad U1 may process information reasonably necessary to provide its services, including transaction, account, advertising, communication, and payment-related information.

Participants must provide accurate information and must not knowingly submit malicious, fraudulent, or unauthorized data.

Participants must not attempt to access another participant's account, circumvent platform security, exploit vulnerabilities, interfere with infrastructure, obtain unauthorized transaction information, manipulate platform records, or conduct other unauthorized technical activity.

Security, privacy, confidentiality, and data-protection obligations are governed by the MSA and applicable policies.

GoldRoad U1 does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free availability of every service.

Temporary limitations may occur because of maintenance, upgrades, security incidents, third-party outages, regulatory requirements, infrastructure failures, network failures, payment-provider restrictions, logistics disruptions, external advertising-platform changes, or force majeure events.

The MSA governs force majeure, liability, service limitations, and related matters.

GoldRoad U1 may take action where a violation is sufficiently established under applicable enforcement procedures.

Nothing in this section prevents a participant from exercising rights available under applicable law or the dispute provisions of the MSA.
