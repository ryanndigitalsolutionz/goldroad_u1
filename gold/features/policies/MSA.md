## 2. Purpose

This Agreement establishes the general contractual framework under which GoldRoad may provide digital, commercial, creative, technical, marketing, advertising, strategic, professional, and related services to Client.

Individual engagements will be described in one or more Statements of Work ("**SOWs**"), proposals, order forms, or other written service specifications accepted by both Parties.

## 3. Current Service Scope

For purposes of this Agreement, GoldRoad U1's active service scope is limited to the services expressly described in this Agreement and the applicable SOWs or other accepted service documents:

1. **Freelancing** — facilitating professional skills, services, and production work.
2. **Advertising** — enabling the creation, management, placement, distribution, or facilitation of advertising and promotional activity.

Services or capabilities outside this scope are not included merely because they may be contemplated as part of GoldRoad U1's future development.

GoldRoad may introduce additional services through a future revision of its contractual and service framework.

GoldRoad U1 is designed as a global remote SaaS commercial platform currently supporting two principal activities:
