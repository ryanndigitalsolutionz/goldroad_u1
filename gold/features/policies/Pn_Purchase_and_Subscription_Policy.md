# GoldRoad U1 — P-n Purchase and Subscription Policy

**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

## 1. Purpose

This P-n Purchase and Subscription Policy governs the purchase, use, refund, and subscription treatment of P-n on GoldRoad U1.

P-n, also referred to as Pin-points, is an internal GoldRoad U1 platform utility used to access eligible GoldRoad U1 services.

This Policy distinguishes between custom P-n bundle purchases and subscription bundles and establishes the conditions under which a custom P-n bundle purchase may be refunded.

## 2. P-n

### 2.1 Definition

P-n is an internal platform utility of GoldRoad U1.

P-n is not a bank deposit, e-money account, cryptocurrency, investment, security, or financial asset.

P-n may be used only for eligible GoldRoad U1 services and functionality made available by the platform.

### 2.2 P-n Purchases

Users may purchase P-n through the payment methods made available by GoldRoad U1.

Each P-n bundle purchase is recorded as an individual transaction.

The user's P-n balance does not represent one combined purchase. Each bundle remains associated with its own transaction record and purchase history.

## 3. Types of P-n Bundles

GoldRoad U1 distinguishes between:

1. Custom P-n bundles; and
2. Subscription bundles.

These categories are treated separately for refund purposes.

### 3.1 Custom P-n Bundles

Custom P-n bundles are individual P-n purchases made by a user outside of a subscription.

A custom P-n bundle may qualify for a refund only when all applicable refund conditions in this Policy are satisfied.

### 3.2 Subscription Bundles

Subscription bundles are provided as part of GoldRoad U1 subscription plans.

Subscription bundles are separate from custom P-n bundles.

Subscription bundles are **not refundable under this P-n purchase refund policy**.

Purchasing, renewing, upgrading, or otherwise obtaining a subscription does not create a refund eligibility window for the P-n or benefits associated with that subscription.

## 4. Five-Minute Refund Eligibility

### 4.1 Refund Window

A custom P-n bundle purchase may be submitted for a refund only within five (5) minutes of the purchase being successfully recorded.

The five-minute period applies to the individual bundle purchase transaction.

Once the five-minute period has elapsed, that purchase is no longer eligible for a refund under this Policy.

### 4.2 Latest-Purchase Requirement

Only the user's latest successfully recorded custom P-n bundle purchase may qualify for a refund.

A previously recorded purchase loses its refund eligibility when another bundle purchase is successfully recorded after it.

The previous transaction remains in the user's transaction history and is not deleted.

The newer transaction becomes the latest logged purchase and is the transaction considered for any subsequent refund request.

### 4.3 Example

A user purchases Bundle A.

Bundle A becomes the latest logged purchase and enters its five-minute refund window.

If the user purchases Bundle B before requesting a refund for Bundle A, Bundle A is no longer eligible for a refund under this Policy.

Bundle A remains visible in the transaction history.

Bundle B becomes the latest logged purchase and receives its own five-minute refund window.

If the user submits a refund request for Bundle B within that window and all other conditions are satisfied, Bundle B may qualify for a refund.

## 5. Unused-Bundle Requirement

A custom P-n bundle must remain completely unused for a refund request to qualify.

For the purposes of this Policy, a bundle is considered used if any portion of the purchased P-n has been:

- spent;
- transferred;
- allocated;
- consumed by a GoldRoad U1 service;
- otherwise used within the platform; or
- otherwise removed from the purchased bundle in a manner recorded by GoldRoad U1.

A bundle that has been used in any manner is not eligible for a refund under the five-minute purchase refund policy.

The refund eligibility check therefore requires both:

1. the purchase to remain within its applicable five-minute refund window; and
2. the purchased bundle to remain completely unused.

## 6. Refund Request Verification

When a user submits a refund request, GoldRoad U1 checks the relevant transaction against its transaction records.

The refund eligibility check determines, among other applicable conditions:

- whether the transaction is the latest logged bundle purchase;
- whether the purchase occurred within the five-minute refund window;
- whether the purchased bundle remains completely unused;
- whether the bundle has been transferred;
- whether the bundle has been spent or otherwise consumed; and
- whether the applicable daily refund-request limit has been reached.

A refund request that does not satisfy the applicable conditions may be rejected.

## 7. Refund Filing

### 7.1 Refund Request

A user may submit a refund request for an eligible custom P-n bundle.

A refund request must identify the individual bundle purchase to which the request relates.

GoldRoad U1 records the refund request as a separate refund event associated with the relevant purchase transaction.

Submitting a refund request does not result in an immediate refund.

### 7.2 Refund Processing

Where GoldRoad U1 confirms that the refund request satisfies the applicable conditions, the refund is filed for processing before the funds are returned.

GoldRoad U1 intends to process an accepted refund within two (2) to three (3) days.

The stated processing period applies to an accepted refund that has not been restrained, suspended, or otherwise placed under review.

### 7.3 Refund Request Limit

A user may submit no more than **one (1) refund request within a twenty-four (24) hour period** under this Policy.

The limit applies to refund requests, not to the number of P-n purchases a user may make.

A new P-n purchase does not reset, extend, or otherwise bypass the user's refund-request limit.

A user may not combine multiple purchases into one refund request for the purpose of bypassing this limit.

## 8. Refund Abuse and Repeated Refund Activity

GoldRoad U1 may restrict, suspend, or place a refund request under review where the platform detects activity that reasonably indicates misuse of the refund system.

Examples may include repeated patterns of:

- purchasing a custom P-n bundle;
- requesting a refund shortly afterward;
- receiving or completing the refund; and
- purchasing another bundle and repeating the same behaviour within a short period.

GoldRoad U1 may also consider the user's previous refund activity, transaction history, timing between purchases and refunds, and other relevant transaction information when determining whether a refund request requires additional review.

### 8.1 Administrative Review

Where a refund request is subject to review, GoldRoad U1 may temporarily restrain the processing of the refund while the relevant transaction history is examined.

A refund request under review is not automatically approved merely because the underlying purchase falls within the ordinary five-minute refund window.

GoldRoad U1 may approve, reject, or continue to restrain the refund request following its review.

### 8.2 Repeated Refund Cycling

GoldRoad U1 may restrict a user's ability to submit further refund requests where the user's transaction history demonstrates repeated purchase-and-refund activity that appears inconsistent with ordinary use of the P-n system.

Such restrictions may include:

- temporarily preventing additional refund requests;
- extending the review of subsequent refund requests;
- restricting refund functionality; or
- suspending refund privileges for a period determined by GoldRoad U1.

These measures are intended to protect the P-n system from repeated refund cycling and do not alter the historical record of completed transactions.

## 9. Effect of a New Purchase

A new custom P-n bundle purchase affects the refund eligibility of the previously latest purchase.

Once a new bundle purchase is successfully recorded:

- the previous purchase is no longer the latest logged purchase;
- the previous purchase's five-minute refund eligibility becomes null and void;
- the previous purchase remains part of the historical transaction record; and
- the new purchase becomes the latest logged purchase.

The previous purchase cannot regain its refund eligibility merely because the new purchase is subsequently refunded or otherwise becomes unavailable.

### Example

A user purchases Bundle A.

Bundle A becomes the latest logged purchase and enters its five-minute refund window.

The user then purchases Bundle B before filing a refund request for Bundle A.

Bundle A's refund eligibility is therefore null and void.

Bundle A remains in the transaction history.

Bundle B becomes the latest logged purchase and receives its own five-minute eligibility window.

If the user later purchases another bundle before filing a refund request for Bundle B, the same rule applies.

## 10. Subscription Transactions

Subscription bundles are separate from custom P-n bundle purchases.

Subscription bundles are **not refundable under this Policy**.

The custom P-n bundle five-minute refund mechanism does not apply to subscription bundles.

A subscription purchase, renewal, upgrade, or downgrade does not create, reopen, extend, or reset the refund window of a custom P-n bundle purchase.

Likewise, purchasing a custom P-n bundle does not create a refund right for a subscription transaction.

## 11. Transaction History and Refund Records

GoldRoad U1 maintains transaction records for P-n purchases and associated refund events.

A transaction that loses refund eligibility because a newer purchase was made is not deleted.

The historical transaction remains available for purposes including:

- establishing the order of purchases;
- determining the latest logged purchase;
- determining refund eligibility;
- recording refund requests;
- reviewing repeated refund activity; and
- maintaining the platform's financial and operational records.

A refund event is likewise retained as part of the relevant transaction history. GoldRoad U1 may temporarily restrain a refund request where the associated transaction activity requires administrative review.

Where a refund request was validly submitted within the applicable five (5) minute refund window, the expiration of that window after submission does not by itself invalidate the filed refund request.

The refund request remains subject to review and the applicable refund conditions.

A refund request that is rejected does not create an additional refund attempt for the same day.

Where a user believes that a refund request was incorrectly rejected or that additional circumstances should be considered, the user may contact GoldRoad U1 through the designated support, helpline, or email channel for further review.

GoldRoad U1 may request a brief explanation from the user where the transaction history indicates a repeated or unusual purchase-and-refund pattern.

**Pin-points have no monetary redemption path. Their only economic direction is INTO the GoldRoad ecosystem and THROUGH eligible GoldRoad services.**

## 12. Refund Review and Processing Status

A refund may move through different internal states, including:

- eligible;
- requested;
- filed;
- under review;
- restrained;
- rejected;
- processing;
- refunded; or
- otherwise closed.

The exact status presented to the user may depend on the state of the transaction and the applicable GoldRoad U1 systems.

A refund being filed or placed under processing does not mean that the funds have already been returned.

## 13. Transaction Charges and Taxes

GoldRoad U1 does not add a transaction tax to the user's P-n purchase.

Payment processing may involve costs charged to GoldRoad U1 by the applicable payment provider.

Such provider costs do not create an additional GoldRoad U1 transaction tax charged to the user.

GoldRoad U1 may therefore account for payment-provider processing costs when operating and administering its refund system.

## 14. Fees and Transaction Tax

GoldRoad U1 does not add a transaction tax to a user's P-n purchase.

The amount presented to the user for a P-n purchase is the amount GoldRoad U1 charges for that purchase, subject to any applicable payment-provider processing conditions disclosed at the time of payment.

## 15. Policy Changes

GoldRoad U1 may update this Policy as its P-n and subscription systems evolve.

Changes to this Policy do not erase or alter the historical record of completed transactions.

The terms applicable to a particular transaction will be determined in accordance with the version of the applicable GoldRoad U1 policies presented to the user at the relevant time.

This Policy forms part of the GoldRoad U1 contractual and operational framework and should be read together with the applicable GoldRoad U1 Terms of Service, Advertising Terms, and other relevant policies.

Where a separate GoldRoad U1 service has specific P-n usage or cancellation rules, those rules may apply to the relevant service transaction.
